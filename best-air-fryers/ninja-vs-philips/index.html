Capacity and basket layout

Philips has more total capacity in this matchup: approximately 9 L / 9.5 qt versus Ninja's approximately 8 qt / 7.6 L. The split matters as much as the total: Ninja offers more symmetrical cooking zones, while Philips pairs a larger basket with a smaller one.

If your main dish takes more space than your side, the Philips arrangement may fit your meal better. If your two components are closer in size, the Ninja arrangement may be preferable. Neither total establishes an exact number of servings: food shape, loading, and the usable space in each basket still matter.

Exact per-basket capacities and dimensions are not documented in the information used here. Check the model specifications for your intended portions and counter space before buying.

Cooking workflow and functions

Ninja DZ201C offers a DualZone workflow with two cooking zones and six functions. Philips NA351/00 offers eight ways to cook and synchronization of its two drawers. Both are relevant when you prepare two meal components; the more symmetrical Ninja layout and the unequal Philips baskets create different ways to divide the food.

A larger number of functions does not establish better cooking performance. The exact function lists and control sequences are not reproduced here; consult the respective manuals if a particular mode or synchronization setting is essential to you.

This editorial comparison does not establish which cooks faster, crisps better, or makes less noise. No comparative cooking times, measured results, noise levels, or power figures are available in the information used for this page.

Cleanup and maintenance

There is no documented model-specific cleanup advantage for either appliance in the information used here. Both have two baskets to consider, but that alone does not tell us which is easier to wash.

Before buying, check each model manual for removable parts, dishwasher guidance, access around inserts, and coating care. We do not assume that either model is dishwasher-safe or has a more durable coating without model-specific documentation.
