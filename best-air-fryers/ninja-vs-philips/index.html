Capacity and basket layout

Philips has more total capacity in this matchup: approximately 9 L / 9.5 qt versus Ninja's approximately 8 qt / 7.6 L. The split matters as much as the total: Ninja offers more symmetrical cooking zones, while Philips pairs a larger basket with a smaller one.

If your main dish takes more space than your side, the Philips arrangement may fit your meal better. If your two components are closer in size, the Ninja arrangement may be preferable. Neither total establishes an exact number of servings: food shape, loading, and the usable space in each basket still matter.

SharkNinja documents two equal 4 QT baskets for the Canadian DZ201C. Philips documents unequal baskets; check the usable dimensions for your cookware and portions. Total capacity does not establish a serving count.

Cooking workflow and functions

Ninja DZ201C offers a DualZone workflow with two cooking zones and six functions. Philips NA351/00 offers eight cooking presets and synchronization of its two drawers. Both are relevant when you prepare two meal components; the more symmetrical Ninja layout and the unequal Philips baskets create different ways to divide the food.

Six Ninja cooking functions and eight Philips food presets describe different kinds of controls; neither count establishes cooking quality. Consult the respective model guides if a particular cooking mode or synchronization setting is essential to you.
