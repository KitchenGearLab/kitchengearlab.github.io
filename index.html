Trust & methodology

How to read our guides and use them well.

We research kitchen gear using documented product information and clear buying criteria. These pages do not report physical product testing. Our goal is not to crown a universal “best”, but to explain trade-offs so you can decide what fits your kitchen.

  • Usability: controls, ergonomics, day-to-day friction.
  • Performance: we do not infer measured results from feature descriptions.
  • Cleanup: residue traps, dishwasher practicality.
  • Durability: no long-term use or physical inspection is reported.
  • Transparency: no unverified claims, no brand worship.

Evidence limits: we have not measured preheating, batch consistency, noise, cleaning time or durability. Recommendations interpret documented features and their practical trade-offs.
