Identify the Plum model precisely

NC601CPU is the exact Plum reference documented by SharkNinja Canada. It should not be silently replaced with NC501C because both carry a Deluxe name. Their listed program selections differ, so compare the controls and included parts rather than relying on a family label.

The useful comparison is the program mix and preparation routine. Check the exact model on the seller listing, then compare included containers, current seller terms and support rather than choosing from colour or the Deluxe name alone.

Choose programs around recipes

Dairy Free, Smoothie Bowl and CREAMiFit are useful distinctions when comparing this second-generation model. They are program names, not a promise that every dairy-free or high-protein mixture will work without following a suitable recipe.

Start with the bases you expect to make most often. If a recipe routine requires more preparation than you will realistically do, extra programs will not solve that mismatch. We have not measured texture, nutritional outcomes, noise, processing speed or durability.

Plan containers, freezing and cleaning together

Two 24 oz tubs let you prepare more than one base, but they also need freezer space and correct freezing conditions. Follow the fill limits and the top/bottom processing instructions. Do not treat volume as a tested serving count.

The manufacturer identifies specified removable food-contact parts as top-rack dishwasher safe. Keep the electrical base out of water, and verify the correct accessory reference when buying spare tubs or a paddle. A matching colour or Deluxe label alone is insufficient.

Questions before buying

How does NC601CPU differ from NC501C?

The Plum NC601CPU is the second-generation Deluxe with a different program mix, including Dairy Free, Smoothie Bowl and CREAMiFit. Both guides document their own model specifications; a new generation is not proof of better measured results.

Is this a soft-serve dispenser?

No. NC601CPU processes prepared frozen bases in Deluxe tubs; NC701C is the dispensing model in this collection.

Does a Dairy Free program remove recipe constraints?

No. Use the manufacturer recipe guidance, fill limits and freezing instructions for a suitable base.
