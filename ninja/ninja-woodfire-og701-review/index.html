Is it right for your kitchen?

Consider Woodfire when outdoor cooking and smoke flavour are the goal. Choose around a suitable outdoor location, and check the exact OG701 bundle and regional support rather than assuming it is identical to OG701C.

A good fit if…

Consider another format if…

Location is the first buying decision

The manufacturer’s OG700 Series instructions require outdoor operation with proper ventilation and clearance. An open window or range hood does not turn a kitchen into a suitable location. Check the guide and the rules that apply to your property before considering the appliance.

This distinction separates OG701 from DG551 in the collection. They are not interchangeable ways to grill in the same place. We do not describe the outdoor model as universally balcony approved or suitable for every apartment.

Pellets provide flavour, not the cooking heat

The unit uses electricity for cooking heat. Approved Ninja Woodfire pellets are used in the smoke system for flavour; they are not a substitute electrical fuel source. Do not use heating pellets, charcoal or unapproved material.

The supplied image includes pellet bags, but a styled composition does not establish the contents of a particular seller bundle. Confirm accessories separately. No measured smoke intensity, cooking yield or comparison with charcoal grilling is claimed here.

Questions before buying

Can I use OG701 indoors?

No. It is an outdoor-only appliance. Follow ventilation, clearance and location requirements in the manufacturer guide.

Do pellets provide the cooking heat?

No. Electricity provides the heat; approved Ninja Woodfire pellets add smoke flavour.

Is OG701 the same reference as OG701C?

SharkNinja lists OG701 and OG701C separately. Shared accessory compatibility does not prove identical seller bundles or Canadian warranty terms; verify the exact offer before buying.
