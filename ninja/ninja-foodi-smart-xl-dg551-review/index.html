Is it right for your kitchen?

Choose DG551 for the indoor grill-and-probe workflow. It does not reproduce a Woodfire pellet-smoke process, and smoke control is not a promise of smoke-free cooking. Its primary specification source is the US model page.

A good fit if…

  • People seeking an indoor grill format rather than an outdoor smoker
  • Cooks who want a documented food-temperature probe system
  • Households that will use both grill and air-crisp configurations

Consider another format if…

  • You need wood-pellet smoke flavour outdoors
  • You expect zero smoke or effortless cleanup
  • You require Canadian warranty coverage without checking seller terms

Advantages and limits of the configuration

These are design trade-offs, not results from physical testing.

Reasons to consider it

  • A wired probe provides a temperature-based workflow for suitable foods
  • Grill and air-crisp configurations offer distinct indoor cooking options
  • Several cooking modes share one countertop appliance

Limits to account for

  • Smoke control does not eliminate ventilation or smoke

The probe is the reason to compare this format

Smart Cook combines a thermometer with protein and doneness selections. That can help organise a temperature-guided cooking routine, provided the probe is positioned and used as instructed. It is not evidence that every portion or cut cooks identically.

We have not checked probe accuracy, measured doneness or tested cooking consistency. Follow the manufacturer guidance and appropriate food-temperature recommendations instead of treating a program label as an unconditional safety or quality guarantee.

Indoor grilling differs from outdoor smoke cooking

DG551 is designed as an indoor grill. OG701 uses an outdoor-only format and a separate pellet smoke system. Choose between them by location and cooking method before comparing the number of functions. Do not add pellets or copy the outdoor smoke workflow into the indoor appliance.

The manufacturer describes smoke-control features, but that should not be rewritten as smoke-free cooking. Ingredients, fat and cooking conditions still matter. Follow ventilation and operating-clearance instructions; no measured smoke or odour reduction is claimed here.
