Is it right for your kitchen?

This is the more substantial glass-air-fryer option in the collection. A 6 QT vessel makes sense when the smaller portable Crispi format feels restrictive, but the single cooking station still determines how you schedule a meal.

A good fit if…

  • Households that want to cook and serve from dedicated glassware
  • Cooks who want adjustable temperature rather than preset-only control
  • One-vessel meals that benefit from choosing between two container sizes

Consider another format if…

  • You need two dishes cooking at independent settings simultaneously
  • You want the smallest portable heating unit
  • You prefer light, stackable baskets over handled glass vessels

Advantages and limits of the configuration

These are design trade-offs, not results from physical testing.

Reasons to consider it

  • Two vessel sizes provide a choice between a smaller batch and a larger main dish
  • Adjustable temperature gives more recipe control than the portable Crispi controls
  • Dedicated glassware supports cooking, serving and storage when used as directed

Limits to account for

  • One cooking station means different settings run sequentially
  • Glass containers need safe lifting, cooling and storage space
  • Six functions do not establish better browning or faster cooking

What changes compared with the portable Crispi?

The Pro uses a countertop base instead of the FN101CGY portable PowerPod arrangement. Its larger vessel and adjustable temperature are the meaningful differences for a buyer: they change the batch size and the control you have over a recipe. They do not turn it into a dual-zone appliance.

Think through a typical dinner. If the main dish and side need different temperatures, stage them or use a second appliance. If everything shares a cooking schedule, serving from glass may be convenient without transferring food.

Choose the vessel around the meal

The 6 QT and 2.5 QT labels describe volume, not a tested portion count. Air frying depends on exposed surface area and spacing, so filling a vessel is different from arranging food for airflow. We have not measured usable cooking area or compared batch results.

Check the exact AS101CCY bundle before ordering additional containers. A compatibility list may include a size sold separately. Do not assume ordinary glass dishes or accessories from another Crispi generation fit this base.

Allow for glass handling and cleanup

Use the dedicated vessels and follow the instructions for cooling, handling and cleaning. Dishwasher-compatible removable parts do not make the electrical base washable. Storage lids are for their intended role, not substitutes for cooking components.

The trade-off is between fewer transfers and substantial glassware to handle. Measure the counter and cupboard space, including room to lift out a hot vessel safely. No cleanup-time, durability or energy-saving claim is made here.

Questions before buying

Can the containers cook independently at the same time?

No. There is one cooking station; two vessel sizes provide a choice of container, not two heating zones.

Is every compatible container included?

No. Compatibility and bundle contents differ. The cited AS101CCY listing specifies 6 QT and 2.5 QT containers; confirm the seller packing list.

Is it proven to cook better than a basket air fryer?

KitchenGearLab has not tested cooking results. The documented differences are the glassware, base, controls and modes.
