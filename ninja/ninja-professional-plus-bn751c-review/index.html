Is it right for your kitchen?

Choose this system for the two container formats and included personal cups. Its 1400 peak-watt headline is not a continuous electrical rating or proof that it blends better than a competing model.

A good fit if…

  • Households that switch between shared batches and single servings
  • People who want two personal cups in the documented bundle
  • Cooks who prefer preset blending programs with manual oversight

Consider another format if…

  • You need a processor bowl for slicing or dough
  • You want a cordless vessel for blending away from a power outlet
  • You intend to use nominal pitcher volume as the liquid fill limit

Advantages and limits of the configuration

  • Two cups can suit a household with separate smoothie routines
  • Presets reduce the need to time every pulse manually

Limits to account for

Choose the container before loading

The pitcher is for a shared batch; a personal cup allows a separate serving without filling the larger vessel. That choice may be more useful than comparing motor headlines. Follow the loading and fill guidance for the particular container.

The 72 oz pitcher label is nominal volume, while the maximum liquid capacity is 64 oz. A cup has its own fill limit. Do not treat those markings as interchangeable or assume a full vessel produces a fixed number of finished servings.

Compare power claims on the same basis

SharkNinja describes a 1400 peak-watt motor and lists a 1200 W electrical rating. Those numbers describe different things. Comparing one blender’s peak claim with another blender’s rated input does not create a reliable performance ranking.

Auto-iQ programs provide a preset pattern of blending actions. They do not demonstrate a particular texture with every ingredient combination. We have not measured ice crushing, fibre handling, sound, motor temperature or durability.

The accessory set is not a processor system

The documented bundle includes a pitcher and two cups, not a separate food-processor bowl. If slicing, dough or a chopping-bowl workflow is essential, look for an appliance and accessory set explicitly designed for it rather than relying on the Ninja family name.

Handle the stacked blade and cup blade according to the owner instructions, including during unloading and cleaning. Check which parts may be washed in a dishwasher and keep the electrical base dry. Sharp removable parts make careful assembly more important than a quick rinse.

Questions before buying

Does BN751C include a food-processor bowl?

The cited bundle lists a pitcher and personal cups, not a food-processor bowl.

Can I fill the pitcher with 72 oz of liquid?

No. The documented maximum liquid capacity is 64 oz; follow the marked limits.

Is 1400 W its continuous rating?

No. 1400 W is a peak claim; the specified electrical rating is 1200 W at 120 V.

The buying decision

Choose BN751C if both a pitcher and two personal cups will be used. Confirm the exact bundle and compare rated power separately from peak marketing claims.
